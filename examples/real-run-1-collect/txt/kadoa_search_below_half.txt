For the first time ever, Google Search is less than 50% of Alphabet's revenue

In the quarter to Sep 2026, Google Search made $63.4B of Alphabet's $128B in revenue, or 49.7%. It is the first quarter below half since Alphabet began reporting Search revenue separately. Search revenue still grew 12% year over year; Google Cloud's grew 103%. In the 31 quarters before, Search was between 52.8% and 62.0%. Over the same years, Google Cloud grew from 4.4% of revenue to 24.1%.

About the data

Search is the line Alphabet reports as "Google Search & other": ads on Google Search and on other Google properties such as Gmail, Maps and Google Play. YouTube ads, the ad network, subscriptions, devices, Google Cloud and Other Bets are counted in everything else.

Search revenue still grew: the share fell because the rest of Alphabet grew faster.
