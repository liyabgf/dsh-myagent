engineering, information retrieval, artificial intelligence, economics, behavioral science, 
and data privacy and data security. No TC member may have a conflict of interest that

unless the Court so approves, no TC member: 
a. may have been employed in any capacity by Google or any 
Competitor to Google within the six -month period directly predating their 
appointment to the TC;

expert in this action; or

during the time that they serve on the TC and for one (1) year after ceasing to serve 
on the TC.
