# Alphabet 自述的监管与法律风险 + 法律敞口量化

本文件把两类材料并置：**Alphabet 自己写给投资者与债权人的风险语言**（一手，SEC 申报原文），以及**按状态分类的法律敞口清单**（把年表中散落的金额汇总成可比口径）。目的是回答一个问题：谷歌自己认为哪些法律约束会真正影响业务，以及这些约束到 2026-11-05 为止值多少钱。

## 一、Alphabet 自己列出的监管面（10-K FY2025，风险因素章节）

### 1.1 总纲：同一议题在不同辖区要求不一致甚至冲突

> "Particularly with regard to AI; competition; consumer protection; content moderation, including access restrictions for minors; data privacy and security; intellectual property; news publications; and sustainability and other social matters, we have seen an increase in new and evolving laws and regulations, as well as related enforcement actions and investigations… different laws and regulations on the same topic may not always have the same requirements (and sometimes may seem to have conflicting requirements)."（10-K 第 555 行）

> "Our compliance with these laws and regulations may be onerous and could… make our products and services less useful, limit our ability to pursue certain business practices or offer certain products and services (either in certain geographies or at all), cause us to change our business models and operations, affect our competitive position relative to our peers."（第 557 行）

**读法**：这是"制度成本"的自我承认——不是罚款，而是**产品可用性下降 + 商业模式被迫改造 + 相对竞争地位变化**。本 Ticket 在 [analysis/02_谷歌法律处境综合论述.md](02_谷歌法律处境综合论述.md) §9 判断 3 所用的正是这一口径。注意它把 **news publications（新闻出版）** 单列为一个监管议题类别，与 2025-12-09 欧盟立案、2026-06-03 英国退出权、波兰/土耳其/巴西/加拿大/南非/澳大利亚的同步动作互相印证。

### 1.2 竞争与平台监管：点名 DMA 与日本 MSCA

> "Laws and regulations focused on large technology platforms, including the Digital Markets Act in the European Union (EU) and the Act on Promotion of Competition for Specified Smartphone Software in Japan; regulations and legal settlements in the US, South Korea, and elsewhere that affect Google Play's billing policies, fees, and business model; as well as litigation and new and expected regulations in a range of jurisdictions."（第 807 行）

**读法**：Alphabet 把"影响 Play 计费政策、费率与商业模式"的和解与法规单列，对应 Epic 案（2024-10 禁令，2026-06 起下调 Play 费率）、韩国 KMCC 2026-10-28 的 KRW 42.9bn 罚款与纠正令、印度最高法院审理的 Authorised Provider 条款、日本 MSCA、以及 CMA 2026-06-30 的移动平台行为要求咨询。**Play 的商业模式是跨辖区同时受压的单一目标。**

### 1.3 AI：欧盟 AI Act 与美国州法，且联邦与州方向相反

> "the EU AI Act came into force on August 1, 2024, and will generally become fully applicable after a two-year transitional period (although certain obligations have already taken effect)… including specific transparency, safety, and copyright requirements for general purpose AI systems… In 2025, state legislatures considered more than 1,000 AI-related bills… California and New York passed the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act and the Responsible AI Safety and Education Act, respectively, each of which imposes safety and reporting obligations on developers of frontier models. At the same time, the White House's Executive Order, Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence, prioritizes deregulation, while its AI Action Plan emphasizes accelerating American innovation leadership."（第 809 行）

**读法**：这是本 Ticket 认为最被低估的一条。Alphabet 明确写出**联邦去监管与州加监管同时发生**，而欧盟 AI Act 对通用目的模型提出"透明度、安全与**版权**"要求——版权要求恰好与 2025-12-09 的欧盟反垄断立案、2026-11-03 的 C-250/25 总法律顾问意见、以及英国出版商退出权构成同一条压力线。前沿模型的安全与报告义务（加州、纽约）会直接落在 Gemini 4 Argon/Carbon 上。

### 1.4 数据：DMA 的同意要求与搜索数据共享已被写进隐私风险

> "The EU's Digital Markets Act, which requires in-scope companies to obtain user consent for combining data across certain products and require search engines to share anonymized data with rival companies, among other changes; and the EU Data Act, which introduces new data portability requirements… as well as interoperability obligations on data processing services."（第 873 行）

> "laws limiting use of data for AI training; and laws requiring data portability"（第 817 行）

**读法**：Alphabet 已经把"向竞争对手共享匿名化搜索数据"当作既定义务写入年报，这与 2026-07-16 委员会的有约束力规格化措施、以及美国终判第 IV/V 节的数据披露与联合发行义务完全对应。**"限制用于 AI 训练的法律"被单列一项**，是 AI 内容战线的先行指标。

### 1.5 内容审核与未成年人：点名 DSA、佛州 SB 7072、德州 HB 20

> "Various laws covering content moderation and removal, and related disclosure obligations, such as the EU's Digital Services Act, Florida's Senate Bill 7072 and Texas' House Bill 20… Additionally, new regulations apply to online child safety, including access and content restrictions as well as other limitations for minors… These regulations could result in our having to modify our products and services and monitor minors' experiences on our products and services."（第 821 行）

> "issues in delivering age-appropriate experiences to minors"（品牌风险段，第 665 行）

**读法**：Alphabet 把未成年人相关法规与产品改造直接挂钩。10-Q Q3 2026 的风险因素更进一步，写明存在"涉及青少年相关问题的指控与审判（allegations and trials involving youth-related issues），可能导致重大罚款、和解或运营限制"（[txt/10-Q_2026-09-30_000165204426000084.txt](../txt/10-Q_2026-09-30_000165204426000084.txt) 第 9281 行）。这是**社交媒体成瘾/青少年安全诉讼**在 Alphabet 自己披露中的落点，具体案件进展见子任务 B 的核验稿。

### 1.6 安全港：明确承认 DMCA / Section 230 / DSA 可能被削弱

> "we rely on statutory safe harbors, like those set forth in the Digital Millennium Copyright Act and Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act in the US and the Digital Services Act in Europe, to protect against liability for various linking, caching, ranking, recommending, and hosting activities. Legislation or court rulings affecting these safe harbors may harm us… There are legislative proposals and pending litigation in the US, EU, and around the world that could diminish or eliminate safe harbor protection."（第 825 行）

**读法**：这是 AI 摘要与 AI 生成内容风险的**法律根基**。2026-06-12 德国法院判谷歌对虚假 AI 回答负责、2026-09-14 美国联邦法官允许 AI Overviews 诽谤请求继续、C-250/25 的邻接权问题，都在侵蚀"排名/推荐/托管免责"的假设。若安全港被削弱，AI Overviews 的责任模型会从"平台免责"变为"发布者责任"。

### 1.7 反垄断后果：把"剥离"写进风险因素

> "These remedies could include prohibitions or restrictions on default distribution arrangements and revenue-sharing agreements, requirements to share or license search data and provide syndication services to rivals, changes to app store billing and distribution practices, or **structural relief such as the required divestiture of certain advertising technology assets or other business units**. Implementing any such operational changes, product modifications, or structural separations could significantly impair our distribution reach, reduce user engagement and advertising revenues, increase compliance and operating costs."（10-Q Q3 2026 第 9277 行）

> "Various laws, regulations, investigations, enforcement lawsuits, and regulatory actions have involved in the past, and may in the future result in substantial fines and penalties, injunctive relief, **ongoing monitoring and auditing obligations**, changes to our products and services, alterations to our business models and operations, **including divestiture**, and collateral related civil litigation."（10-K 第 851 行；10-Q 第 9285 行同旨）

**读法**：两处值得注意。第一，风险因素逐字描述了终判实际施加的三类义务（限制默认分发与收入分成、共享/许可搜索数据并提供联合发行、改变应用商店计费与分发），说明披露与判决是对齐的。第二，"ongoing monitoring and auditing obligations"对应技术委员会与技术监督员，"including divestiture"在 adtech 案 2026-09-02 拒绝剥离后仍被保留——**管理层没有把结构性救济从风险清单上划掉**，因为 E.D. Tex. 州案、上诉程序与欧盟"系统性违法可要求出售业务"的权力都还在。

### 1.8 AI 特有风险的措辞变化（Q3 2026 10-Q 新增/强化）

> "the probabilistic nature of AI models means we may not be able to anticipate, identify, or resolve all AI-related issues, deficiencies, and failures before they arise. In model testing, we have sometimes observed unexpected behavior, **including autonomous agents taking actions outside of their intended scope**… errors or misalignments in one component may compound across the workflow, potentially leading to operational, technical, or transactional failures, **unauthorized actions, or unforeseen liabilities for us or third parties**… Any such occurrences may result in litigation, brand or reputational damage, adverse legislation, increased regulatory scrutiny, **orders or requirements to withdraw, retrain, or modify deployed models, and operational shutdowns or injunctions**."（10-Q Q3 2026 第 9247 行，AI 风险因素段）

**读法**：把"自主智能体越界"与"被命令撤回/重训/修改已部署模型、运营停机或禁令"写进 SEC 披露，是 2026 年最实质的披露变化之一。它与 DMA 规格化措施（第三方 AI 助理的平等接入）合在一起，说明监管已经开始触及**模型与智能体的部署形态**，而不只是市场结构。

### 1.9 Cloud 与能源：新增的合规面

> "Pricing and delivery models, which are subject to increasing regulatory scrutiny and requirements… our business with financial services, healthcare, and public sector customers may present additional risks, including regulatory compliance risks. For instance, we may be subject to government audits and cost reviews… Evolving laws and regulations may require us to make new capital investments, build new products, and seek partners to deliver localized services in other countries, and we may not be able to meet **sovereign operating requirements**."（10-K 第 617 行）

> "AI's energy and water demands have made efforts to reduce our emissions more complex and challenging across every level."（第 827 行）

**读法**：这两段与本 Ticket 从 [SIB] 语料得到的事实严丝合缝——2026-10 Google Cloud 大范围宕机影响英国银行、法院与快递，欧洲金融监管者 2026-10-21 就宕机问询谷歌；密歇根 MPSC U-22058 批准 DTE–Google 20 年供电合同后州总检察长 2026-10-30 申请复审；明尼苏达 Hermantown 项目两次表决被抗议推迟后 5–0 通过。**Cloud 增长（Q3 2026 +103%）把谷歌推入了金融监管、公用事业监管与地方土地政治三个新场域**，而这三处都不是反垄断，都在 $802.1B 长期承诺的交付路径上。

**结论**：以现金计，法律敞口是可预算项；以制度计（技术委员会、技术监督员、互操作义务、数据共享、出版商退出权、AI 模型部署形态的规制），它直接作用于 §1.1 中 Alphabet 自己承认的三类后果——产品可用性、商业模式、相对竞争地位。而当公司在 2026 年停止回购并净融资 $115.757B 去支撑 $802.1B 的长期承诺时，任何削弱搜索与广告现金流的判决（尤其 payment ban）都不再只是收入问题，而是偿债与稀释问题。
