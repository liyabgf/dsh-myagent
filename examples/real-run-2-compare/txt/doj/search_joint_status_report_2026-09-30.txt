JOINT ST
ATUS REPORT 
Pursuant to the Court’s July 31, 2026, Minute Order, the Parties submit the following Joint 
Status Report. 
The Parties jointly propose that they file a further status report on August 14, 2026. 
I. Compelled Testimony from TC Staff 
On July 31, 2026, the Parties met and conferred regarding Google’s comments on 
Plaintiffs’ proposed language for a prohibition on compelled testimony from TC Staff.  
Plaintiffs 
then conferred with the Technical Committee (“TC”), which also co nferred with its counsel.  On 
August 5, 2026, Plaintiffs and the TC’s counsel held a call to discuss the issue, and today Plaintiffs 
sent revised proposed language to Google. The parties will continue to confer.

II. Google’s In
demnification of TC Staff 
On August 3, 2026, the TC’s counsel sent Google an email identifying certain items to be 
addressed regarding indemnification by Google of TC Staff and re lated insurance issues, as well 
as written responses to questions raised by Google during a call on July 27, 2026.  On August 5, 
2026, Plaintiffs, the TC’s counsel, and Google held another call to discuss further details on these 
issues. 
Plaintiffs and Google are currently awaiting a written proposal from the TC’s counsel on 
indemnification and related insurance issues, which the TC’s counsel anticipates sending early 
next week. Once Plaintiffs and Google receive th at proposal, they will promptly meet and confer. 
III. Post-TC Employment Restric
tions 
On July 30, 2026, Plaintiffs made a revised proposal to Google concerning the application 
of the post-TC employment restri
ctions in Section VII.A.2.c of  the Final Judg ment, which the 
Parties also discussed during their July 31 meet a nd confer.  Plaintiffs thereafter conferred with 
the TC, and on August 4, 2026, Plaintiffs sent a revised proposal to Google.  The Parties met and 
conferred again on August 5, 2026, regarding that pr oposal.  The parties will continue to confer, 
and if they are unable to reach agreement, propose to file their competing positions with the Court 
on August 11, 2026.
